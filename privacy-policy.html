Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Spoonme app (the "App") for mobile devices, created by PeakStreak, Inc. (referred to as "we" or "us") as a Freemium service. This service is provided "AS IS."

Information Collection and Use

When you download and use the App, we collect certain information. This might include:

  • Your device’s Internet Protocol (IP) address. We log requests to troubleshoot issues. We are not interested in your data outside of resolving these problems.
  • The resources you visit in the App, including the time and date of your visit and the time spent on those pages. We don’t currently capture this data, but we might in the future.
  • Search terms or barcodes you use when searching for food, to help us ensure we return the right results and improve our search functionality. We want to create the best possible search experience for our users. Collecting search results where we didn’t find any results is important in helping us improve.
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device. We collect this data as part of normal request logging, but it gives us insight into how many versions back of our software it makes sense to support.

Location Information

We don’t gather precise location information about your mobile device. Some of our logging reveals the area your request originated from, but we have no interest in this data outside of normal troubleshooting activities.

Personal Information

To enhance your experience, we may ask for personally identifiable information such as your email, username, first name, and last name. This information will be retained and used to personalize your experience of using Spoonme, or to use your first name in the greeting in our emails to you. We do not sell this data to third parties.

We may use the information you provide to contact you with important information, required notices, and marketing promotions.

Third-Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically sent to external services to help us improve the App and our service. We may share your information with third parties as described in this privacy policy.

Please note that the App uses third-party services with their own privacy policies. Here are the links to their privacy policies:

  • RevenueCat for managing Apple subscriptions.
  • App Signal we use App Signal to help us understand errors that are occurring in our application and similar issues.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the App. Use the standard uninstall process available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace.

Data Retention Policy

We will retain user-provided data for as long as you use the App and for a reasonable time afterward. If you’d like us to delete your user-provided data, please contact us at support@spoonme.kitchen and we will respond in a reasonable time. We agree with 37signals that you should treat people as well when they are leaving your product or service as when they are joining. We will try to make leaving as pleasant and frictionless of an experience as possible for you.

Age Restrictions for Our Application

We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. Our founders don’t let their kids even have phones before the age of 13. We don’t think it’s healthy for kids to have social media, or food tracking apps, at that young of an age. Spoonme does not try to get anyone under the age of 13 to sign up. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@spoonme.kitchen so we can take the necessary actions.

Security

We are committed to safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. We provide physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information we process and maintain. We want you to be able to fully rely upon us to have a safe, great experience using our product. Please let us know if you are aware of any potential issues. We will work hard to address them swiftly and thoroughly. Reach out at support@spoonme.kitchen.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes by updating this page with the new privacy policy. Please check this privacy policy regularly for any changes, as continued use of the App means you accept any updates.

This privacy policy is effective as of 03 July, 2024. The first person who actually reads this gets a year’s subscription for free.

Your Consent

By using the App, you consent to the processing of your information as described in this privacy policy now and as amended by us.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy while using the App, or have questions about our practices, please contact us at support@spoonme.kitchen.
