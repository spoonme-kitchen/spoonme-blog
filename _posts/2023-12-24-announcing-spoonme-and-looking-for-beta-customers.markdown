## Announcing spoonme.kitchen. A social food app that enables you to be deliberate about you food.

`The problem:` Food tracking apps on the market today are focused on weight loss, big returns for investors, or macronutrients. This meets the needs of some customers, but leaves many of us underserved. We want something that better aligns with our needs and values. We don't want a weight loss focused app. We don't want a glorified spreadsheet to track our food. Food is social and intricately connected with culture and relationships! That is worth calling out again.

# FOOD IS SOCIAL!

Food is community. It is culture. It's memories of sitting around a table with our friends or loved ones sharing a meal and warming our soul. It's about that time when you couldn't stop drinking this one ginger tea with your friends for months because you were all obsessesed.  Spoonme was created to celebrate community, culture, and your loved ones. We are releasing features that we think are a great _first step_ in achieving these goals. Let's chat through the ones we are releasing today:

  - **Share the food with the people you love.** Easy sharing features to send recipes to your followers' "inbox" for them to add to "cookbooks"
  - Share the food you are eating to the **social feed**
  - Effortless **food tracking** for deliberate eating. 

Existing food experience apps are lacking. We want to build an entirely new experience around you and the food you eat. Some of our best memories are eating with the people we love. Finding a new dish at a restaurant and trying to recreate it at home. **_Food is social_**. We want to give you the ability to share the food you love with those you love and be deliberate about what you are eating. Here is a bit more depth about each ability we are providing in this `0.1` release.

### Share recipes direclty with those you love, without any of the hassle

You know the drill. You search through Instagram, Pinterest, Youtube, or Tiktok to find the recipe and where you saved it. You then open up messages and paste the link to your friend. Then they go figure out where they want to save it. We are tired of this hassle. You need something better. 

Spoonme enables you to psuh and hold the `share` button in the interface, select a person to send the recipe to (frequents or favorited are at the top), and you are done. This recipe then shows up in the `Inbox` of your friend or lover's Spoonme account, and they can assign it to a cookbook.

### See your friend's latest food find at a local restaurant, or a great recipe they tried out, in the social feed

We all have that friend who is trying out the best new restaurants in town and we want to see every. damn. picture. Or our brother in law (hi @a-a-ron!) is always trying out new recipes he finds and sending us pictures. We want this to be effortless to discover new food and to save it to our cookbooks. When tracking a food you can choose (always opt in) to share that picture to your followers in their social feed. That way they can simply follow you and see the amazing things you are cooking up, or the great restaurant that just opened. 

You can also go to a user's profile and see all of the food they've eaten that they have made public. Some will only post certain photos to their feed, others will share their entire food journal. That is up to them. All sharing is opt in. But if they do share, you can see what they are eating and find some great new meals to try out!

### Effortless tracking of your food to be deliberate about the food you are eating

We all have those friends who can eat intiuitively and have no issue being deliberate. Others of us (our founder @mattcantstop included!) need to be a bit more deliberate. For all of us mere mortals, we are creating the most effective and flexible food tracker on the market. This is a work in progress, but we are excited to share our progress with you today. 

We have the ability to create recipes based on specific ingredients and provide flexible tracking for those recipes. We also have over 400,000 foods in our database for you to easily track what you are eating. We will be working on improving the functionality for food tracking with each week's release. Barcode scanning is our top priority at this point. We know that's an essential tool to have for most users. What are some of your most needed functionality for food tracking?
