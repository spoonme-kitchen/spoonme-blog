# Food is life. The rest is just details. 

We are animals, after all. We want to eat! Food is at the heart of many of our best memories, closest moments, and the connecting thread of cultures and communities. Food can bridge cultural divides, calm and mend a broken relationship, and be a comfort meal during dark times. But the tools we use to manage the food in our lives are unimaginative at best, and feel like backend administration tools for a business at worst. We believe we can create an experience that is the connecting thread for the many ways food impacts us and enriches our lives. Wwe call it Spoonme. We just launched in the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spoonme/id6483933170). This simple (and needing polish!) release is just the beginning. We have big plans!

![App View](/assets/images/app-view.jpeg)

## Our food tracking app

Spoonme's first release is something that we believe is the foundation of a good food experience: a food database and a way to track your food. Many of us find ourselves eating foods we don't love because they are nearby and convenient. We eat less of the food we love, and we often have our health suffer as a result, because we are not deliberate enough. Spoonme's food tracking application is our first area of focus to solve this issue. Our database is in its infancy, but we are working hard to make it the best food experience on the market. 

### Building blocks for your meals or recipes

Our food database enables you to build up meals to track, or recipes to create, with actual ingredients with nutritional values. Use actual ingredients with their FDA values, or products like an oreo cookie for a cookie crust. With Spoonme you can more easily get accurate data than relying on guess work. 

And, when you just want to track something quickly, you can always use Quick Entry mode (just a name, optional photo, and your best guess at calories). Quick Entry works great when you are out with friends and don't want to spend much time entering values, enabling you to come back to it later in the day. 

We plan to add some innovative features down the road based on these building blocks that make entering your data even faster. We believe this is a great foundation to help you now, while enabling us to do some creative things later. 

### A visual photo grid view to better _feel_ how you are eating

Most apps are just a list of the things you've eaten. Like we said, an admin tool like feel. At Spoonme we want you to see what you are eating as an overview. You can more intiuitively reason about whether you've fueled your body well for the day. You can always switch back to list view if you prefer. 

![Grid view](/assets/images/grid-view.png)
### On the fly calorie calculation

Spoonme's food tracker also lets you calculate calories on the fly as you are entering your recipes. Adjust the unit amount and see the calories adjust in real time. This is great when the measurement size is unfamilar, but you have a good guess about what your total calories are for that food. 

## Areas for improvement

Our goal was to get this tool **released and in your hands so we can get your feedback and iterate to an incredible product.** We feel strongly that for a subset of people using a competing tool, Spoonme will better serve their needs. We are busy working on adding some more polish and features to the tool. We are focusing on these areas in coming weeks and months: 

- Small bug fixes
- Improving our "Add a Food" feature. We want to enable you all to augment our database. We will be adding support for:
  - Adding restaurants and making ordering from restaurants the best experience on the market. We are targeting being able to select menu items from each restaurant and track them directly. 
  - Augmenting our barcode data in the foods that exist and creating new ones
